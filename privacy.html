Privacy policy

Countries of the World: Learn & Quiz · Learn Logic · Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version

Countries of the World does not ask for your name, email address or any account. Your progress stays on your own device and is never sent to us.

The app is free because it shows adverts from Google AdMob, on both Android and Apple devices (iPhone and iPad). The adverts are never personalised, and only adverts suitable for all ages are requested.

Who we are

Countries of the World is published by Learn Logic, a trading name of Star Analytical Services Ltd, a company in the United Kingdom. Contact: staranalytical1+Countries@gmail.com.

What the app saves on your device

To remember how you are getting on, the app saves on your device: the countries you have marked as learnt, your test scores and history, your best streak, which countries you have answered right or wrong, and your settings (such as the colour scheme, sound, vibration and test options).

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not shared with anyone.

Adverts (Google AdMob)

A full-screen advert may appear at most once every 10 minutes of using the app, and only at a natural break — such as between screens or at the end of a test — never in the middle of a question.

To show adverts, Google AdMob collects some information from your device: a device or advertising identifier, your IP address (which gives a rough location, such as the country), basic device details, and how adverts are seen and tapped. Google uses this to deliver adverts, limit how often you see the same one, measure them, and prevent fraud.

The app only ever asks Google for non-personalised adverts, which are based on the app itself rather than on your activity in other apps or websites, and only for adverts rated suitable for all ages. The app does not track you, so it does not show Apple's "Allow tracking?" message.

If you are in the UK, EU or Switzerland, a consent message from Google appears when you first open the app. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Advert privacy choices.

Google's use of this information is explained in Google's privacy policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Deleting your data

You can delete your progress at any time in the app with Settings → Reset all progress. Deleting the app also deletes all of its saved data.

To reset the advertising identifier used by Google, use your device's settings (Privacy → Ads on Android, or Privacy & Security on iPhone and iPad).

Age

Countries of the World is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Emails to us

If you email us for help, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we do not share them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you and ask us to delete it. As the app keeps your progress only on your device, the only information we could hold is an email you have sent us. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
