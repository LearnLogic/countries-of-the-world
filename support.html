Help & support

Answers to common questions about the Countries of the World app. We welcome any feedback to help us improve it.

How do I learn the countries?

Open Learn, pick a region (or All countries) and go through the countries. Each one shows the world map with a close-up, the country’s key facts and an amazing fact. Tap "Mark as learnt" when you know a country — or turn on automatic marking in Settings → Learning.

What kinds of test are there?

Choose Multiple choice (4 or 5 answers — set this in Settings) or Type the answer. Each question shows a country on the map. You can test any region, only the countries you have learnt, or the ones you keep getting wrong.

Does the typed answer have to be spelt perfectly?

No. Small spelling slips are accepted, and common alternative names work too — for example "UK", "Holland" or "Ivory Coast".

Can I get a hint in tests?

Yes. Turn on Settings → Tests → Show map in tests to see where each country is. Your results show which tests were taken with the map hint, so you can compare your scores with and without it.

Can I change the order of the countries?

Yes. In Settings you can choose A–Z or Random, separately for learning and for tests.

Can I change the colours?

Yes. Settings → Colour scheme has five schemes to choose from.

Why are there adverts?

Adverts keep Countries of the World free. You will see at most one every 10 minutes, and only at a natural break — never in the middle of a question. The adverts are never personalised and are suitable for all ages.

Can I turn the sounds or vibration off?

Yes. In the app's Settings there are separate switches for Sound and Vibration. Sounds are also silent when your device is on silent.

Will my progress move to a new phone or tablet?

Not at the moment. Progress is saved only on the device you use, and deleting the app deletes your progress too. If there is enough demand for this feature, we will add it — so let us know if you would like it!

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Reset all progress. Deleting the app also removes all of its data. See the privacy policy for more.
