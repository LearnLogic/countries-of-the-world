Countries of the World: Learn & Quiz

An app by Learn Logic

Learn about all 196 countries, region by region. Each country is shown on a world map with a close-up of where it is, plus its flag, capital, languages, area, population, GDP per person, currency and an amazing fact.

Then test yourself: find each country on the map, with multiple choice or by typing the answer. Track how many countries you have learnt, see your scores and streaks, and practise the ones you find tricky.

Free, with occasional adverts. No account needed — your progress stays on your device.
